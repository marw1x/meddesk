Inside the program

What it does

Realistic tooth chart

Crowns, implants, bridges and root canals as they look in the mouth, with more than one condition per tooth. Plus a primary-teeth chart.

Appointments and instant follow-up

Book into free slots. The follow-up is booked into the calendar the moment you type its date in the visit.

Clear payments and balances

Full, partial or outstanding payment, in local currency or dollars at the rate you set, with a one-click receipt.

Imaging and CBCT discs

Images are linked to the tooth they show, and a CBCT disc is copied whole into the patient file with its viewer.

Lab work and surgery

Track each lab case with its shade and due date. Surgery is its own session type, and suture removal is booked straight after.

PC and tablet together

An Android app for tablet or phone joins the clinic PC over the clinic network. Reception opens the visit and the doctor finds it ready.
